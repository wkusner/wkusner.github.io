Version 0.2 · updated 2026-10-03

How we choose

  • Free to use, or free with a basic account.
  • From a reputable source: an open-textbook project, a university, a professional society, or a public agency.
  • Actively maintained, with no paywall in the middle of the material.
  • Clear enough that a student could use it alone at midnight.
  • Each entry says what it is good for and where to start.

Using this page

Links open in a new tab. The QRC doesn't run these sites. If a link is broken or a resource has changed, tell us at the QRC desk.

Calculators and solvers are for checking and exploring. Follow your course's policy on what is allowed for graded work.

A plain, printable version is at static.html.
