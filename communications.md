### PC-2015-1 {#pc-2015-1}

**Source.** S. Akiyama and J. Caalim, *Invariant measure of rotational beta expansion and Tarski's plank problem*, [arXiv:1509.04785](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.04785), Acknowledgments.

**As published.** Thanks to Peter Grabner and Wöden Kusner, "who informed us of the result of Bang," which gave Theorem 1.1 its present form.

**Contribution.** Bang's solution of Tarski's plank problem: if a convex body is covered by planks, the sum of their widths is at least the minimal width of the body (T. Bang, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 2 (1951), 990–993).

### PC-2020-1 {#pc-2020-1}

**Source.** R. Kusner and P. McGrath, *On Steklov eigenspaces for free boundary minimal surfaces in the unit ball*, Amer. J. Math. 146 (2024), 1275–1293, [arXiv:2011.06884](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.06884), Corollary 4.2(ii); reference "W. Kusner. Personal communication, 2020."

**As published.** "We are grateful to W. Kusner for pointing out [12] that (i) implies (ii)" (arXiv); Acknowledgments (AJM): "Wöden Kusner for pointing out how Corollary 4.2(i) implies 4.2(ii)."

**Contribution (Corollary 4.2(ii)).** For an embedded free boundary minimal surface Σ ⊂ B³ of genus zero, each component of ∂Σ is a convex curve in S².
